# Stock overview

Mode: Operate

Read the latest WTC.AX daily price, inspect a trading date, or compare time ranges. Show Yahoo Finance market and financial figures with their currency and availability.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep the price series on one continuous field with a compact ledger beside it.

OWN-WORLD: A rail timetable's dark ink display, clear route trace, fixed readings and cool daylight frame. Hairlines separate measurements. Type is compact with tabular numbers.

STORY: Identify the security, read the price and date, then scrub the history or scan the ledger.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead, company header, then a wide dark chart alongside the light market ledger. The range controls sit directly above the plot. On mobile the ledger follows the chart.

FORM: Railway timetable, third candidate, f92c3a2f.
