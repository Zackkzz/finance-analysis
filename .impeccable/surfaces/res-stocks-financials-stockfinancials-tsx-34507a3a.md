# Ticker financials

Mode: Operate

Compare the income statement across reporting periods for the selected ticker. The overview and ticker search remain within reach.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Put the reporting periods side by side in one ledger, with the metric labels fixed during horizontal scrolling.

OWN-WORLD: Extend Market brief's blue-grey canvas, paper panels, condensed headings and teal controls. Use tabular figures and hairline rules.

STORY: Identify the ticker, start with Annual or switch to Quarterly, then compare revenue, earnings and supporting line items across reported dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared masthead and ticker navigation, then the company identity above a wide income statement. A navy date band aligns the first figures beneath it. On narrow screens the table scrolls within its panel.

FORM: Code-led extension of the existing Railway Timetable system.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
